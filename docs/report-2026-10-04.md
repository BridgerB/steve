## 10. Open problems, ranked by expected minutes saved per race

There is no race funnel this cycle, so these are computed from the gym funnel, per natural run. A run stands in for one bot's portal phase; a race has three bots.

1. **Lava deaths during the cast — about 7 min per run directly, more in lost frames.**
   - 1.90 h of dispatch time ended in death across 17 runs.
   - 13/17 runs [53%, 90%] record lava as their death cause, and 18 of 19 base3 dispatch deaths were lava.
   - The deaths happen in the anchor phase (6), at the lava pour (4), at verify (3) and at lava_fill (3), all within reach of the pool.
   - Fix candidates, all staged: 7.1, 7.4 (`lava_safe_move`) and 7.3 (the station).
2. **Frames abandoned and recast — at least 2.3 min per run, plus 9/10 frames lost.**
   - In base3, 54 obsidian events against a lower-bound best-frame total of 40 means at least 14 blocks were cast into frames the bot then abandoned: 14 × 99 s median per block ≈ 23 min over 10 runs.
   - "no anchor (far)" fired in 7 of 10 base3 runs (70% [40%, 89%]) and 10 of 16 runs counting p0b ([39%, 82%]).
   - Root cause: the anchor cell is the frame's corner, so with the left column cast there is obsidian at its head height and the bot can never stand within 1.5 of it.
   - Fix: `scripts/cycle4/01-frame-origin.ts`, which is first in the queue. Two 9/10 frames (base1-1, base3-2) would have needed one more block.
3. **Re-anchoring a new site near an existing frame.** base3 logged 39 `site_anchor` events in 10 runs (2–8 per run). A `site_reuse` that falls through (no lava bucket) re-anchors 1–3 blocks off, abandoning the frame's obsidian. That waste is part of the 23 min above. The fix is not written: never re-anchor within 16 of a frame that has obsidian; refill and resume instead.
4. **Per-block time on natural ground: median 99 s, p80 313 s, against about 37 s on the arena.** About 60 s per block × 10 blocks ≈ 10 min per portal if it reached arena speed. Fix candidates: 7.2 (front-load lava), 7.3, then R2.
5. **"lava not exposed" and anchor-range refusals.** Together that is 37 failed dispatches (23 + 14). These are fast failures, so the cost is small in minutes but large in dispatch count.
6. **Obsidian lost from a frame.** One cell was cast 3× in p0b-6. The cause is undiagnosed; `01-frame-origin.ts` adds an `obsidian_lost` event.
7. **False "frame already present".** It turns a run into a guaranteed fail at the walk-in. It happened once (base3-6).

## 11. What I would tell the Rust agent

- **Death does not mean the site is gone.** Ending the attempt on death is right, but forgetting the site is not. Mine threw away a 9/10 frame (base1-1: 20 obsidian cast, no portal). Keep the site and walk back if it is within 200 blocks.
- **The landing spawnpoint is not reliable.** `/spawnpoint` succeeds (server log confirms), but a later respawn can still go to world spawn, presumably when the cell is obstructed. If a gym relies on respawning near the landing, check the respawn position and move the bot back.
- **World spawn is a polluted place on a shared gym server.** Old frames, spilled lava and the same pool keep getting reused: 3 lava deaths at one cell in p0b.
- **Frame-origin trap.** If the frame is anchored at a corner cell that a cast column covers at head height, any "am I on the anchor" test fails once that column is up. Take the origin from stored state, not from the bot's position.
- **"Frame already present" checks that count obsidian within a radius** are fooled by abandoned frames nearby.
- **A gym dispatch loop needs the 4th-identical-failure rule too.** base2-1 burned 102 dispatches on "Pickaxe worn out during site prep" inside the gym loop, which has no planner to escalate to.
- **Placement by forceload + `execute if loaded` + heightmap tp** replaced `spreadplayers` this cycle with no harness landing failures.
- **typecraft's `exclusionAreasStep` was declared but never applied by the pathfinder.** If the Rust SDK ported that config, check that it actually takes effect.

## 12. What the learning system made easier and harder, and decisions needed

**Easier.**
- Every dispatch now has a row with a reason, a phase, a death cause and the landing. Questions that took a trace-reading script last cycle are one query now, and the failure-reason table in section 2 came straight from the log.
- The stall cut and per-dispatch budget removed silent hangs: the longest dispatch was 641 s.
- Recording seed and landing made it possible to see that deaths were all respawning at the same spawn-area pool.

**Harder.**
- The new semantics (2700 s per run) make each natural run take 45 minutes. A 10-run baseline is 7.5 hours. Two restarts for harness faults cost most of a day. The schedule assumed 2 hours.
- The plumbing itself introduced a bug, the forget-on-death, that corrupted a baseline. It was found only because a run cast 20 obsidian and still failed.
- More dispatches per run means more opportunities to re-anchor and abandon frames. The guard surfaces the waste but does not prevent it.

**What I would not do again.**
- I would not ship a death handler that discards state without a unit test that walks the death-then-resume path.
- I would not run a baseline before a single trace review of a "frame at 9/10 then abandoned" run. The frame-origin bug was visible in p0b-6, two batches earlier, and I queued it behind the baseline instead of fixing it first. That kept the baseline "current code" as the doc asks, but it measured a known bug for 7.5 hours.

**Decisions needed from the strategist.**
1. **Fix order.** Fix the frame-origin bug (staged, `01-frame-origin.ts`) before 7.1, ahead of the doc's Part 7 order? I recommend yes: it affected 10 of 16 runs and directly lost two 9/10 frames.
2. **Baseline comparisons.** Should the baseline for compare.ts be base3 (10 runs, these semantics, with the frame-origin bug), or a re-run after the frame-origin fix? Re-running costs another 7.5 hours.
3. **Natural batch size.** At 45 min per run, the doc's "one change per batch with an arena regression between" allows about 2 natural batches a day. Options:
   - a shorter natural cap for screening (e.g. 1800 s), with 2700 s only for confirmation
   - 6-run natural batches
4. **Race timing.** Run the race before the cast reaches any natural pass? The doc wanted one by hour 20 regardless. It did not happen.
